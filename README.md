# Deco My Tree message ideas

121 short Christmas messages, sorted by who they are for, from [Deco My Tree](https://decomytree.link), the online Christmas tree your friends decorate with secret notes.

Use them on a friend's tree, in a card or in a group chat.

## Files

- `ideas.csv`: two columns, `for` and `message`
- `ideas.json`: the same list as JSON

## Categories

- Best friend: 12
- Friends: 12
- Crush: 12
- Partner: 12
- Family: 12
- Funny: 12
- Short: 15
- Compliments: 12
- Work and school: 10
- New Year: 12

## A few examples

- **Best friend:** Of all the trees in the forest, I’m so glad mine grows next to yours. Merry Christmas, bestie! 🎄
- **Partner:** Every Christmas with you feels like the first one. I love you more than all the lights on this tree. ❤️
- **Family:** Mom, thank you for making every Christmas feel magical. I see all the work behind it. ❤️
- **Funny:** I was going to get you a real gift, then I remembered this one is free. Merry Christmas! 😅
- **Work and school:** Thanks for being the best desk neighbour anyone could ask for. Happy holidays!

## About Deco My Tree

Deco My Tree is a free website: make a Christmas tree, share one link, and friends hang gifts with notes that open on December 25. It works in 19 languages and friends need no app or account.

- Make your tree: [decomytree.link/create-tree](https://decomytree.link/create-tree)
- More ideas on the site: [decomytree.link/deco-my-tree-message-ideas](https://decomytree.link/deco-my-tree-message-ideas)
